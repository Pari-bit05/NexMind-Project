About StudyBuddy

Problem: dense textbooks, little personal practice and last-minute revision make engineering subjects harder than they need to be.

Solution: an AI study assistant that explains, quizzes and plans, built for first- and second-year engineering students.

Built with: HTML, CSS, JavaScript and a Vercel serverless function. Claude explains topics and powers the tutor chat, Gemini generates quizzes and revision plans, and Perplexity finds current study resources with sources.
